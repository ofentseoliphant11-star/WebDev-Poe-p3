Who We Are and What We Do

Our Approach

Brownstone Landscapes by William Brown is a landscaping & gardening business based in Bloemfontein CBD, Bloemfontein. To provide an evaluation on site of the client’s garden or area, we ask questions such as: does your garden reflect your personality? Is the interior of your office or home in balance with your garden? We discuss the vision, as well as the client’s budget.

Our Services

Thorough garden clean-up, installation of all bricks, sticks and stones that create expectation and flow, focus, and planting of all that green and grow. Being on-site twice per day throughout the implementation phase implies that all planting and installation happen according to plan.

Our Promise

Once our co-created vision of a landscaped garden has been realised, we invite you to make use of Brownstone Landscapes — we are the best gardening service in Bloemfontein.
