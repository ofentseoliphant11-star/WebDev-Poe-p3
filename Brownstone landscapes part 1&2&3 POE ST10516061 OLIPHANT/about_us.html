    • Our Story

    Brownstone Landscapes provides professional landscaping and garden design services to clients across the region.

    Founded on the belief that every home deserves a garden that feels like a natural extension of the property, we combine years of landscape design experience with a deep respect for nature. Under the leadership of William Brown, our team works closely with each client to understand their lifestyle, aesthetic preferences, and long-term vision.

    We do more than plant trees or lay patios; we architect outdoor environments where families gather, children play, and lasting memories are made. Whether you are starting from a blank canvas or refreshing an established garden, we are dedicated to bringing your outdoor dream to life.
